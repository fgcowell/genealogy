Research

Mical Cowell of early Philadelphia was not born in 1672 on the Isle of Wight.. The record of his arrival in Philadelphia shows he arrived in 1733 at age 31, thus born in 1702. No other record of his birth, life, or death exists; his family relationships, and what became of him, are unknown.
Many trees show Mical Cowell as the father of Christian Cowell of Hunterdon County, NJ, but this is impossible. The birth dates of Christian Cowell's children and grandchildren indicate that he could not have been born later than about 1715, and likely even earlier, making a father-son relationship between these two impossible.
There are also some trees that show Mical Cowell's wife as Elizabeth Ann Hufty, born 28 Apr 1672 in Coggeshall, Essex, England and who died 1720 in Hunterdon, New Jersey. This too is a fiction. There was an Elizabeth Cowell, daughter of a Mark Cowell and his wife Ann, who was born 28 Apr 1672 in Coggeshall, Essex, England. But this Elizabeth Cowell had nothing to do with Christian Cowell of Hunterdon, New Jersey. And there was an Elizabeth Cowell who died 13 Sep 1720, but she died in St. Botolph Aldgate, London, England, and again, she had nothing to do with Christian Cowell of Hunterdon, New Jersey.
