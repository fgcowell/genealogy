Event Date Place Description Sources
Immigration 1766 Yagodnaya Polyana (or “Berry Meadow”), Saratov, Russia from Ober Lais, Hessen 1b 2a
Note: 1

The family joined the immigrant transport to Russia in nearby Büdingen in the summer of 1766 and was among the eighty families from the region who established the Volga German colony of Yagodnaya Polyana the following year. They sailed from Lübeck, Germany to Oranienbaum near St. Petersburg on September 13, 1766, and likely passed through Moscow en route to the Volga.

Note: 2

Hartmann Schauermann, his wife Elisabeth, his children (Johann Conradt, age 20; Joh. Jost, age 18; Joh. Henrich, age 14; Hans Henrich, age 12; Henrich, age 6; Balthasar, age 5; Elisabeth, age 2; Anna Elisabeth, born en route) are recorded on the list of colonists being transported from St. Petersburg to Saratov in 1767.

They settled in the Volga German colony of Yagodnaya Polyana on 16 September 1767. They are recorded there on the 1767 Census in Household No. 26.

Note: 3

The Oranienbaum passenger list records that Johann Hartmann Scheuermann and his family came from the German region of Darmstadt. The 1767 census of Yagodnaya Polyana records that he came from the German region of Nidda, while the 1767 census of Frank records that Johann [Kaspar] Scheuermann came from the German village of Lais in the region of Hessen-Darmstadt.
