Having trouble connecting parents of John L Squier. Several users believe father is Daniel Squier (b. 6/8/1784 Vermont d. 3/4/1879 Wisconsin) and mother is Sarah Ann Lytle (b. 12/22/1782 Rhode Island d. 6/7/1847 Wisconsin). But I can't find any concrete evidence linking them, and there are several Daniel Squier/Squire/Squires living in New York all around the same time (especially on 1810 census).
On the 1880 Minnesota Census linked to Miles, Emerson and John, John's parents birthplaces are listed as Nova Scotia (Father) and New York (Mother). So that contradicts the above information as well, unless the information is incorrect on the census.
The only thing I have which links a John Squier to a Daniel Squier (and mother "Lytle" is a Minnesota death index but the person listed is John "M" Squire, not John L. So that makes me think it might be one of the other families. I am unable to view the actual image to verify the information on the record. I think this might be where everyone else has made the connection to Daniel and Sarah. But I remain unconvinced. Nevertheless I linked that record as a citation to this family, for future reference.
