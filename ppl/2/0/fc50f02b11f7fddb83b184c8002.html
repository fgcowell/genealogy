Obituary
From FindaGrave (https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/66835058/helen-harriet-chaney)

Mrs. Chaney died May 11, 2001, in Roseburg, OR. She was born September 15, 1914 in Hughson, CA and moved with her parents to Sutherlin, OR in 1923.

She was a member of Eastern Star in Oakland, OR and enjoyed traveling, gardening, music and various hobbies.

Survivors include her husband, Howard Chaney; one daughter, Arlene (Cowell) Blackwell and husband Don of Bend, OR,; one son, Delvin Squier and wife Janabel of Redmond, OR.; grandchildren, Dean Cowell and wife Sandi of Bend, OR, Dennis Cowell of Bend, OR, Jerry Blackwell and wife Chris of Chandler, AZ, Darren Squier and wife Angela of Redmond, OR, Steve Squier and wife Tina of Highlands Ranch, CO, Toby Squier and wife Sabrina of Prineville, OR,;step daughter Pamela Wilds and husband Edward of Umpqua, OR.; step grandchildren Nathan and Erin Wilds of Umpqua, OR. Mrs. Chaney is also survived by 12 great-grandchildren and two brothers, Bud Holm and wife Vera of Sutherlin, OR and Norman Holm and wife Marge of Vancouver, WA.

Mrs. Chaney was preceded in death by her parents, Elijah and Sarah Holm, one brother, Ivan Holm, and two husbands, Floyd Squier and Richard Kemp.

A memorial service will be held at the First Methodist Church, 181 E. Second Street in Sutherlin, OR on Saturday, May 19, 2001 at 3 p.m. Private inurnment will be held in the Valley View Cemetery in Sutherlin at a later date.

(Obit published in the Roseburg, OR "The News-Review")
